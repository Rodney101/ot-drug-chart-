OT Drug Chart — User Guide

OT Department · Drug Management System

Overview

The OT Drug Chart is a web-based application used by the OT department to manage drug stock levels and log usage. It connects to Google Sheets in real time so all staff see the same up-to-date information across any device.

Accessing the app

Open the app in any browser using the link provided. On Android phones it can be installed as a home screen app for quick access.

DASH — Dashboard

Gives a quick overview of the current state of the drug chart.

Metrics
  • Drugs tracked — total number of drugs on the chart
  • Low stock — drugs at or below their reorder threshold
  • Critical / out — drugs with zero stock remaining
Recent activity

Shows the last 8 actions — drug usage logs, stock additions, updates, renames, and removals. Pulled from Google Sheets on every sync so it reflects activity from all staff on all devices.

Sync status bar

The bar below the header shows the connection status and last sync time. Tap "Sync now ↺" to manually refresh.

The app syncs automatically on every page load and every 5 minutes.
LOG USE — Recording Drug Usage

Use this tab every time a drug is administered.

Steps
  1. Tap the LOG USE tab.
  2. Select the drug from the dropdown.
  3. Use − and + to set the quantity.
  4. Add a note if needed — optional.
  5. Tap Log usage.
Stock decreases immediately. If it drops to or below the reorder threshold, an alert fires and an email is sent automatically.

Adding a drug
  1. Tap Add drug.
  2. Type the name — tap a suggestion if it appears.
  3. Enter current stock and reorder threshold.
  4. Tap Save drug.
Editing a drug name

Tap the blue pencil icon → edit in popup → tap Save. Updates both app and Google Sheets.

Removing a drug

Tap ✕ → confirm. Removes from app and Google Sheets. A "Drug Removed" entry is added to the Logs tab.

You can also delete a drug directly from the Stock tab in Google Sheets — the next sync removes it from the app automatically.
ALERTS — Stock Alerts

Lists every drug that is low or out of stock. The alert count shows on the tab badge.

  • LOW — stock is at or below the reorder threshold
  • OUT — stock is zero, reorder immediately
Check the Alerts tab before ending your shift.
Google Sheets Integration

The app connects to a Google Sheet as its central database. All changes are written automatically. Stock levels pull back on every page load and every 5 minutes.

  • Logs tab — full audit trail of every action
  • Stock tab — current drug list. This is the source of truth

Changes on one device appear on other devices after their next sync. Worst-case lag is 5 minutes.

Important Notes
  • Log drug usage immediately — do not batch log at end of shift.
  • If "Sync failed" shows, you may be offline. Logs save locally and sync when connection restores.
  • Tap "Sync now" for the most current stock counts across devices.
  • Drugs deleted from Google Sheets are removed from the app on the next sync.
